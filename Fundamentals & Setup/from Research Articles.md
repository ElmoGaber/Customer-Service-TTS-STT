### ✴️ “The Future of Translation: How STT & TTS Are Changing Multilingual Communication” (Evelyn Stefani, 2025)
- Highlights the global communication revolution driven by STT & TTS.
- Key insight: **AI + accessibility** → multilingual inclusivity.
- Future direction: **AI-powered personalised voices**, **real-time translation**, **scalable voiceovers**, and **human-AI collaboration**.

### ✴️ “Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech (2025)”
- Emphasizes how **deep learning** improved accuracy.
- Introduces **EVI 2** and **Octave** as unified STT/TTS systems.
- Key takeaway: **End-to-end learning → higher naturalness, lower latency.**

### ✴️ “Free Text to Speech Models: Best Open Source TTS Solutions in 2025” (Peter Song)
Top free and open-source TTS frameworks:
1. **Tacotron 2** – High quality, slower inference.
2. **FastSpeech 2** – Real-time capable, fast and efficient.
3. **VITS** – End-to-end, expressive and multilingual.
4. **Coqui TTS** – Easy fine-tuning, multi-speaker.
5. **ESPnet-TTS** – Research-level multi-task speech framework.

Each can be combined with **HiFi-GAN** or **WaveGlow** vocoders for lifelike speech output.
